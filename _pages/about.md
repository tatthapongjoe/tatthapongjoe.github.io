## About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial and Systems Engineering at Texas A&M University, advised by Dr. Jaesung Lee.

My research focuses on developing domain knowledge-guided machine learning methods for inference and decision-making in complex engineering systems. My work integrates physics-informed machine learning, probabilistic modeling, and reinforcement learning to address challenges in inverse problems, uncertainty quantification, and intelligent maintenance planning.

I am particularly interested in applying these methods to computational mechanics, material characterization, reliability engineering, and factory automation.

I expect to complete my Ph.D. in 2027 and welcome opportunities for international research collaboration.

## Research Interests

- Physics-Informed Machine Learning
- Inverse Problems and Computational Mechanics
- Probabilistic Modeling and Uncertainty Quantification
- Reinforcement Learning and Intelligent Maintenance
- Reliability Engineering and Factory Automation
